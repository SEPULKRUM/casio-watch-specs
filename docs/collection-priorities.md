## Identity and evidence

Record JF/JR/ER and other observed market codes as evidenced regional references; do not manufacture duplicate physical variants by appending market suffixes. Original and U generations remain separate even where a regional sales page reuses an old model name. Do not propagate case, band, mass, display polarity or module assignments from a neighboring model.

No protected listing was fetched. Images must retain source/rights provenance; discovery does not establish reuse permission. Official product pages and manuals supply specifications; exact-model support pages can corroborate variant existence and module links. Unavailable product pages remain unresolved even if support confirms the name. Preserve existing and verified data.
