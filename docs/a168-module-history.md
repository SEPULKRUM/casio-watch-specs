# A168: old module references and the 3298 transition

Research checked 15 November 2026. **2012 is the best-supported provisional introduction year for the 3298 version, not a proven worldwide manufacturing cutoff for 1572.** The exact production switchover remains unresolved. No current catalog module assignment is changed by these findings.

## What the sources establish

| Date | Source | Finding | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1995 | [Casio's official retrospective](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/50th/Heritage/1990s/) | A168 family introduction | Does not identify the original module or every regional SKU |
| 3 November 2009 | [Archived Casio Europe A168WA-1YES page](https://web.archive.org/web/20091103065142/http://www.casio-europe.com:80/euro/watch/collection/a168wa-1yes/) | Manual link uses MODULE=1275 | Earliest capture found for this URL; not an original-release page or proof of the fitted movement |
| 2012 | [PccParts model record](https://www.pccparts.com/model.cfm?action=list_part&back=0&mfg=Casio&mode=auto&model_id=A168WA-1+%283298%29) | A168WA-1 (3298) is labelled Year 2012 | Supplier's model-year label, not a manufacturer change notice |
| 2012 | [Casio Operation Guide 3298](https://www.casio.com/content/dam/casio/global/support/manuals/watches/pdf/32/3298/qw3298_EN.pdf) | Visually confirmed copyright 2012, print code MA1203-EA | Copyright date is not a first-production date |
| 8 November 2013 | [Archived Casio Europe page](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108005750/http://www.casio-europe.com/euro/watch/collection/a168wa-1yes/) | Still links MODULE=1275 | Old support pointers can persist after a product revision |
| 28 August 2014 | [Archived Casio Europe page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140828000740/http://www.casio-europe.com:80/euro/watch/collection/a168wa-1yes/) | Links MODULE=3298 | Direct proof of the webpage association by that capture date |

The 2011, January 2012 and September 2013 captures also link 1275; the January 2016 capture links 3298. The observed webpage change is therefore between 8 November 2013 and 28 August 2014. It does **not** date the factory change to 2014.

The older [A168WA-1YES retailer record](https://www.watch.co.uk/casio-a168-series-digital-silver-watch-a168wa-1yes.htm) lists module 1572 and a 2010 Spring/Summer catalog year. That supports an older 1572 association, but the Wayback pages inspected do not explicitly say 1572. Their 1275 manual target is recorded exactly, without converting it into a fitted-module claim.

## Display and backlight reports

The [owner comparison thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/casio/comments/1mvpyko/) reports better head-on contrast for an older example, but includes a modified case and unsupported dating claims. It is an anecdotal report, not a controlled comparison of new, equally powered, unmodified units. No measured contrast or EL-brightness ranking was found.

Casio's 3298 guide explicitly specifies EL backlighting. A quantitative claim that 1572 is brighter or sharper is not promoted to a database specification.

## Database treatment

Retain historical versions separately from current model assignments. Record 2012 as a provisional lead and the archived webpage dates as source-specific observations. Do not replace the existing A168WA module reference with 1275 merely because an old page links its guide. Do not use the current Japanese regional SKU's 2021 release metadata as the initial release of module 3298.

Capture timestamps, original URLs, body hashes, exact manual links and uncertainty are preserved in [the research record](../research/a168-module-history-2026-11-15.json). No complete archived pages, images or supplier parts tables are republished.
