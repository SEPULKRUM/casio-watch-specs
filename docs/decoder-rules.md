# Casio model-code interpretation rules

Every search displays this distinction: the sources reviewed do not provide a complete universal Casio letter-by-letter key. Some readings follow watch-community naming patterns. Official model features and proposed letter expansions are labelled separately. A confirmed Bluetooth feature does not by itself make a B expansion an explicit Casio definition.

Use the longest family prefix, preserve individual-letter positions, and scope interpretations to the relevant model line and era. Unknown meanings remain unknown. The supplied infographic is secondary guidance; its optional-letter suggestions do not become universal rules. In particular U does not universally mean solar, and MV/GMV revision theories do not establish fitted module references.

## GPW-1000 and GPW-2000

- G: G-SHOCK brand association.
- P: Positioning/GPS, with Pilot as an alternative community interpretation.
- W: Wave Ceptor association; individual-letter expansion unverified.

Casio identifies GPW-1000 through its [GPS Hybrid Wave Ceptor support](https://support.casio.com/wat/hybrid/) and documents GPW-2000's GPS, radio reception, Bluetooth and solar features on its [official model-line page](https://gshock.casio.com/europe/products/gravitymaster/gpw-2000/). The decoder keeps the official technology wording separate from suggested acronym readings. It does not present “GPS Positioning Wave” as Casio terminology.

Wave Ceptor is radio-controlled timekeeping terminology; Multi-Band 6 is a particular radio-reception system. Do not treat those names as interchangeable universal prefix definitions. GPW-2000 also demonstrates that a model can have Bluetooth without a visible B feature marker.

## GMW-B5000

Separate GMW + B + 5000, and any later configuration suffix and variant code. M = Metal and B = Bluetooth are scoped community associations; the [cited GMW-B5000D-1 page](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/gshock/product.GMW-B5000D-1/) independently confirms metal construction, Bluetooth, Multi-Band 6 and Tough Solar. Band material remains variant-specific. A later D or other configuration code is not given a universal material definition.

## GW-B5600BC-1BER

| Token | Role | Explanation | Evidence treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| GW | Family | G-SHOCK / Wave Ceptor community reading | Community interpretation; cited watch's solar/radio features confirmed separately |
| B | Feature / series modifier | Bluetooth association in this line | Feature confirmed; letter expansion remains community interpretation |
| 5600 | Series | Model-series identifier | Not a module or production date |
| BC | Series configuration suffix | Bracelet / composite-band variant reading | Scoped community interpretation; cited band confirmed |
| 1 | Colour code | Black manufacturer colour category | Exact cited model's Casio metadata |
| B | Additional variant / order marker | Further variation within this colour/configuration code | Exact meaning unverified; not automatically Bluetooth |
| ER | Market suffix | European-market reference | Not the manufacturing country |

The [official European page](https://www.casio.com/europe/watches/casio/product.GW-B5600BC-1B/) displays GW-B5600BC-1B and records European SKU GW-B5600BC-1BER in product metadata. It gives Black as the colour category and confirms a stainless-steel/resin composite band, Bluetooth, Multi-Band 6 and Tough Solar. These facts support this example; they do not prove a universal BC acronym or assign a meaning to the later B.

The same scoped example accepts compact input `gwb5600bc1ber`. Unknown suffixes stay unverified. The colour/order split also preserves strings such as 1A2 as colour digit 1 plus additional marker A2, without inventing a universal blue or material meaning for A2.

## DW

Display Digital Water Resistant as a watch-community interpretation. DW is used on G-SHOCK and non-G-SHOCK models. The [official DW-291H-1AV page](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/casio/product.DW-291H-1AV/) provides a standard Casio example with a 200 m rating. That does not establish every DW model's rating, an official acronym expansion, or a permitted diving depth.
