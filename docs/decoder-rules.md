# Casio model-code interpretation rules

Every search displays this distinction: the sources reviewed do not provide a complete universal Casio letter-by-letter key. Some readings follow watch-community naming patterns. Official model features and proposed letter expansions are labelled separately. A confirmed Bluetooth feature does not by itself make a B expansion an explicit Casio definition.

Keep the longest family prefix together as one token and one model-family row. Do not display individual-letter meaning cards. Scope interpretations to the relevant model line and era. Unknown meanings remain unknown. The supplied infographic is secondary guidance; its optional-letter suggestions do not become universal rules. In particular U does not universally mean solar, and MV/GMV revision theories do not establish fitted module references.

## GPW-1000 and GPW-2000

- G: G-SHOCK brand association.
- P: Positioning/GPS, with Pilot as an alternative community interpretation.
- W: Wave Ceptor association; individual-letter expansion unverified.

Casio identifies GPW-1000 through its [GPS Hybrid Wave Ceptor support](https://support.casio.com/wat/hybrid/) and documents GPW-2000's GPS, radio reception, Bluetooth and solar features on its [official model-line page](https://gshock.casio.com/europe/products/gravitymaster/gpw-2000/). The decoder keeps the official technology wording separate from suggested acronym readings. It does not present “GPS Positioning Wave” as Casio terminology.

Wave Ceptor is radio-controlled timekeeping terminology; Multi-Band 6 is a particular radio-reception system. Do not treat those names as interchangeable universal prefix definitions. GPW-2000 also demonstrates that a model can have Bluetooth without a visible B feature marker.

## GMW-B5000

Separate GMW + B + 5000, and any later configuration suffix and variant code. M = Metal and B = Bluetooth are scoped community associations; the [cited GMW-B5000D-1 page](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/gshock/product.GMW-B5000D-1/) independently confirms metal construction, Bluetooth, Multi-Band 6 and Tough Solar. Band material remains variant-specific. A later D or other configuration code is not given a universal material definition.

## GW-B5600BC-1BER

| Token | Role | Explanation | Evidence treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| GW | Family | G-SHOCK / Wave Ceptor community reading | Community interpretation; cited watch's solar/radio features confirmed separately |
| B | Feature / series modifier | Bluetooth association in this line | Feature confirmed; letter expansion remains community interpretation |
| 5600 | Series | Model-series identifier | Not a module or production date |
| BC | Series configuration suffix | Bracelet / composite-band variant reading | Scoped community interpretation; cited band confirmed |
| 1 | Colour code | Black manufacturer colour category | Exact cited model's Casio metadata |
| B | Colour variation within -1B | Blackout / Black Accent community reading; checked negative LCD and darker dial treatment versus -1 | Community interpretation; appearance observed separately; not the Bluetooth modifier |
| ER | Market suffix | European-market reference | Not the manufacturing country |

The [official European page](https://www.casio.com/europe/watches/casio/product.GW-B5600BC-1B/) displays GW-B5600BC-1B and records European SKU GW-B5600BC-1BER in product metadata. It gives Black as the colour category and confirms a stainless-steel/resin composite band, Bluetooth, Multi-Band 6 and Tough Solar. These facts support this example; they do not prove a universal BC acronym. The later B is now explained using the scoped -1B blackout interpretation described below.

The same scoped example accepts compact input `gwb5600bc1ber`. Unknown suffixes stay unverified. The colour/order split also preserves strings such as 1A2 as colour digit 1 plus additional marker A2, without inventing a universal blue or material meaning for A2.

## DW

Display Digital Water Resistant as a watch-community interpretation. DW is used on G-SHOCK and non-G-SHOCK models. The [official DW-291H-1AV page](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/casio/product.DW-291H-1AV/) provides a standard Casio example with a 200 m rating. That does not establish every DW model's rating, an official acronym expansion, or a permitted diving depth.

## Recurring -1B blackout / black-accent pattern

For GW-B5600BC-1B / European SKU GW-B5600BC-1BER, the B after colour digit 1 is part of the variation code -1B. Show **Blackout / Black Accent** as the watch-community reading, distinguishing it from the Bluetooth B before the series. In this checked pair, the -1B product image has light digits on a dark background and darker dial accents. The -1 image has dark digits on a light background with yellow/gold accents; blue is not recorded for that cited photo.

The original GW-M5610-1 / -1B and updated GW-M5610U-1 / -1B pairs provide two additional checked examples of positive -1 versus negative -1B display treatment. “Blackout” does not mean every printed marking or component is literally black. Casio-image observations are labelled separately from the proposed letter-name expansion; viewing an image does not prove that Casio officially expands B as Blackout or Black Accent.

For an unreviewed -1B model, explain the recurring community pattern but leave actual display polarity unverified. It is not universal: the official F-91WM-1B image shows a positive LCD, and AE-1200WH-1BV likewise has a light-background main LCD. Exact checked observations take precedence over naming patterns. Other B positions and suffixes do not automatically acquire this meaning.

The eight selected image observations, source-page and image checksums are recorded in [the observation note](../research/negative-variant-observations-2026-11-15.json). Images were inspected locally only; none is added to GitHub, embedded in the website or hotlinked. These decoder observations do not alter specification-catalog or module records.

## BC suffix boundaries

BC is separated structurally wherever it ends the series portion, even when the
model lacks a specification record. For example GWF-D1000BC-1 splits into
GWF / D / 1000 / BC / 1. That parsing alone does not establish the exact model
or a bracelet configuration. GW-M5610BC-1 splits into GW / M / 5610 / BC / 1;
GW-M5610UBC-1 retains the scoped Updated U before BC. Original and compact input
forms agree. Unknown meanings and unconfirmed model identities remain labelled.


## Whole-prefix presentation

The model-family row now carries the supplied guide's whole-prefix meaning under
**The community understands this prefix as:**. For AE, show **Digital multi-function /
world-time family** with the guide's PDF page 3 citation and a community label.
AE stays one prefix; the decoder must not render separate A and E cards.

Whole-prefix guide readings cover Wave Ceptor, Lineage, BABY-G, Classic/Standard,
AE/AEQ, MDV-related families, MTP/LTP and CMK. Earlier community examples GW, GMW,
GPW and DW are also shown as grouped readings. Unknown or historical-collision
contexts keep their qualified reference description. Model-series, BC/U, colour,
order and market segments remain separate as before. Official feature observations
and community meanings retain their separate classifications.


### M before 5600 / 5610 in GW squares

For GW-M5600 and GW-M5610 (including GW-M5610U), show **M — Multi-Band**, specifically the Multi-Band 6 reception association, as a scoped community interpretation. Six transmitters: Japan (two), United States, United Kingdom, Germany and China. Casio module [3159](https://support.casio.com/storage/en/manual/pdf/EN/009/qw3159.pdf) and [3495](https://support.casio.com/storage/en/manual/pdf/EN/009/qw3495.pdf) manuals document reception; they are feature evidence, not an official acronym definition.

The owner supplied the naming-history association with the earlier GW-5600 generation. Earlier radio coverage varies by exact regional model; no universal band count is assigned to GW-5600 here. M inside GMW retains its metal interpretation, B in GW-B5600 its Bluetooth interpretation, and U remains a separate Updated marker. Do not extend this rule to unrelated M series.
