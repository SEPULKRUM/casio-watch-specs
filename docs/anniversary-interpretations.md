## GWF-D1035B-1

Display **GWF | D10 | 35 | B | 1**, with a final regional suffix when supplied. D10 is a community segmentation linking this commemorative designation to GWF-D1000; B is the owner-supplied black / special-colour interpretation. Casio's support feed identifies a 35th Anniversary FROGMAN, module 3445, 59.2 × 53.3 × 18.0 mm, 141 g, ISO 200 m diving resistance, Tough Solar, radio Multi-Band 6 and gold/red/black colours. These observations do not establish official letter expansions.

Sources: [Casio support](https://www.casio.com/jp/watches/gshock/support.GWF-D1035B-1/), [public Japanese product feed](https://www.casio.com/content/casio/locales/jp/ja/products/product.data.GWF-D1035B-1.json), [module 3445 manual](https://www.casio.com/content/dam/casio/global/support/manuals/watches/pdf/34/3445/qw3445_EN.pdf).

The supplied November 2017 release month is retained; the support feed carries 2017-11-19, while secondary release reports list November 10 and Casio's US announcement describes December availability. Do not treat any of those as a universal worldwide launch date.

JR / CR / DR are owner-supplied market readings. Only the Japanese -1JR SKU was primary-source checked. No region-wide specification equivalence is established. CTL1616, detailed coatings, engraving and hardware descriptions remain owner-supplied claims where not confirmed by the checked sources.

## Guard against false anniversary parsing

Do not infer an anniversary from any arbitrary number ending in 25, 30, 35 or 40. Directory matches show a supplied collection annotation. The special digit split is implemented only for GWF-D1035. SS / ZE and other thematic suffixes are described only for the exact listed models; they are not universal suffix definitions. GWF-1035F has a different base family and must not inherit the D1000 depth-sensor rule.
