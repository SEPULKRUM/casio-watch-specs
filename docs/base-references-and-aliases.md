# Base references, shorthand aliases and support fallbacks

The main search now distinguishes base-family research from individual colorway references. When a clean base code has indexed color-specific children, its record is attached as family context under those children. It is not another variant row. Its original facts remain accessible through direct links and the family/base filter, including owner specimen measurements. If no children exist, a base-only record remains accessible as before. Every original identifier remains in the audited raw index.

`research/model-identity-rules-2026-11-17.json` records the owner-reported DW-5200 duplicate spellings and scoped reference-only vintage-family spelling mappings against the supplied heritage inventory. There is no global stripping of A or C. DW-5000C-1A and -1B remain different variants. The DW-5200C -1 black/red and -9 gold references remain distinct. Aliases do not acquire canonical model specifications in their source records. Existing regional grouping and conflict guards continue to apply.

Model-number queries now prioritize matching identifier strings, rather than listing unrelated watches whose shared module/source notes merely mention that number. Material, function and numeric module searches retain fact-based matching. Pagination, sidebar counts and hero counts use the same grouped results. Family-only matches show a family context rather than an invented variant.

The owner's four exact Casio support URLs are used only when a verified exact product/specification link is unavailable. They are labelled support/manual references and do not promote specification verification. Explicit aliases may link to the clearly labelled canonical support target. Support URLs are never guessed. The policy and exact owner-provided links are recorded in `research/official-support-fallbacks-2026-11-17.json` and generated with `official_support.py`. Identity rules are generated with `model_identity.py`; both support `--check`.
